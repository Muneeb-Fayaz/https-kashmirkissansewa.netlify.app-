## 📌 About the Project

**Kashmir Kisan Saathi** is a farmer-focused digital platform designed to connect farmers, buyers, cold storage operators, transporters, processors, and administrators through one simple ecosystem.

The project aims to make agricultural trade more transparent, accessible, and user-friendly by helping farmers discover genuine buyers, compare offered rates, access cold storage, arrange transport, reduce post-harvest losses, and explore better selling opportunities.

---

## 🎯 Problem Solved

Farmers often face difficulties such as:

- Finding genuine buyers
- Comparing different buyer offers
- Getting fair prices for produce
- Accessing cold storage facilities
- Arranging transportation
- Reducing post-harvest losses
- Finding processing and value-addition opportunities
- Managing produce listings and orders
- Communicating with different service providers

**Kisan Saathi brings these services together on one platform.**

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 👨‍🌾 Farmer Dashboard
- Add and manage produce
- View interested buyers
- Compare buyer rates
- Contact buyers directly
- Receive cold storage purchase offers
- Book cold storage
- Book transport
- Participate in group selling
- Track produce and related activity

### 💰 Interested Buyers & Rates
Instead of relying only on fixed market prices, farmers can view **real offers from interested buyers**.

Farmers can compare:
- Buyer name
- Produce required
- Offered price
- Quantity
- Location
- Contact details

This improves price transparency and gives farmers more selling choices.

### ❄️ Cold Storage Value Hub
Cold storage operators are more than storage providers.

They can:
- Offer storage services to farmers
- Buy produce directly from farmers
- Grade produce into Grade A, B, C, Processing Grade, etc.
- Set grade-wise selling prices
- Publish graded stock
- Sell graded produce to buyers
- Arrange transportation

### 🛒 Buyer Marketplace
Buyers can:
- View farmer produce
- View graded produce from cold storage
- Compare available stock
- Place purchase requests
- Contact sellers
- Track orders
- Buy directly from farmers or cold storage operators

### 🚚 Transport Services
- View available transporters
- Contact transport providers
- Request transport
- Cold storage operators can also book transport
- Helps move produce safely from farm or storage to buyer

### 🏭 Processing & Value Addition
Users can explore processing opportunities such as:
- Juice
- Jam
- Dried products
- Other value-added agricultural products

This can help reduce waste and increase the value of produce.

### 👥 Group Selling
Farmers can:
- Combine produce
- Sell larger quantities
- Attract bigger buyers
- Improve bargaining power

### 🤖 Kisan Saathi Help Assistant
A floating help robot is available across the website.

It provides:
- Built-in common questions
- Instant answers
- Website guidance
- Help related to farmers, buyers, storage, transport, login, and orders
- Option to message the admin for further support

The help icon can also be moved around the screen for easier access.

### 🌐 User-Friendly Design
- Simple navigation
- Large buttons
- Clean dashboards
- Mobile-friendly layout
- Smart Board responsive improvements
- Multilingual-friendly structure
- Easy-to-use interface

## 🔄 How It Works

```text
Farmer
   ↓
Adds Produce
   ↓
Views Interested Buyers & Rates
   ↓
Chooses Best Opportunity
   ↓
Sell Directly to Buyer
        OR
Sell / Store in Cold Storage
   ↓
Cold Storage Can Grade Produce
   ↓
Grade A / Grade B / Grade C / Processing Grade
   ↓
Graded Stock Listed for Buyers
   ↓
Buyer Places Order
   ↓
Transport Arranged
   ↓
Produce Reaches Buyer
```

---

## 🧊 Cold Storage Business Flow

```text
Farmer → Cold Storage → Grading → Buyer
```

Example:

```text
Farmer has produce
        ↓
Cold Storage purchases or stores it
        ↓
Produce is graded
        ↓
Grade A / Grade B / Grade C / Processing Grade
        ↓
Cold Storage sets grade-wise prices
        ↓
Buyer purchases the required grade
```

This helps improve quality transparency, reduce waste, and create additional selling opportunities.
